**Last updated: 7 August 2026**

This is the privacy policy for **Transcribe Offline**, a macOS application by
David Silva.

## The short version

Transcribe Offline collects nothing, transmits nothing, and stores nothing about
you. It has no network access at all. Every file you open is processed on your own
Mac and never leaves it.

## What is collected

**No personal data is collected.** Specifically, the app does not collect, store,
transmit, or have access to:

- your name, email address, or any account details — there is no account, and no
  sign-in;
- the audio and video files you open, or any part of their contents;
- the transcripts the app produces;
- usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostics;
- advertising or tracking identifiers;
- your location, contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera.

The app displays no advertising, contains no third-party analytics or tracking
software, and makes no in-app purchases.

## Why this is enforced rather than promised

Transcribe Offline runs inside Apple's App Sandbox, and it is **not granted the
network entitlement**. That is a technical restriction applied by macOS, not a
policy statement: the app is unable to open a network connection, whether or not
it wanted to. It is also unable to read any file on your Mac other than the one
you explicitly hand it.

The speech-recognition and speaker-identification models are bundled inside the
application. Nothing is downloaded on first launch or at any point afterwards.

## Your files

When you open a video or audio file, the app decodes it in memory and produces a
transcript on screen. Nothing is written to disk unless you choose to export it,
and then only to the location you pick in the save dialog.

The app does not keep a history, a cache of past transcripts, or a record of which
files you have opened.

## Data shared with third parties

None. There are no third parties. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed, because
none is collected in the first place.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages, collects no data from anyone, and contains no
user-generated content, social features, or links to external services from within
the app other than the credits in **Transcribe ▸ Acknowledgements**.

## Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights of access, correction, erasure and portability
over personal data held about you. There is no personal data held about you, so
there is nothing to access, correct, erase or port. If you would like that
confirmed in writing, ask.
