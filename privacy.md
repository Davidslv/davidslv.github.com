**Last updated: 18 August 2026**

This is the privacy policy for **davidslv.uk**, a personal site run by David Silva.

## The short version

The site is a static brochure. It does not have accounts, and it does not store a
database of readers. The only personal data I collect is an email address (and,
if you type one, a name) when you subscribe to the Engineer's Notebook, plus the
anonymous usage totals Cloudflare records so I can tell whether a page is being
read.

## Newsletter

The signup form on this site posts to [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com), who
process the subscription on my behalf. What they receive:

- your **email address** (required);
- a **name**, only if you type one;
- a **source tag** (which page you subscribed from) and a GDPR consent flag;
- the confirmation that you clicked the double-opt-in link.

I use that to send the Engineer's Notebook (about once a fortnight) and any
lead magnet promised on the form you submitted. I do not sell the list, and I
do not send anyone else's mail on it.

You can unsubscribe from every email in one click. You can also email me and I
will delete the record.

**Processor:** Buttondown, Inc. Their own policy is at
[buttondown.com/legal/privacy](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy).

## Analytics

The site loads a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. It is cookieless and does not
identify you. It tells me page views, referrers, and countries as totals, not as
a profile of a person.

There is no advertising network, no retargeting pixel, and no Google Analytics.

## What is not collected

- no accounts, passwords, or payment details — I do not take payment on this site;
- no comments;
- no precise location, contacts, or device identifiers beyond what a normal
  HTTPS request carries.

Amazon links on the book pages may carry an Associates tracking tag once that
account is active. Amazon then processes the click under
[their own privacy notice](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=201909010).
I never see the purchaser's name or address from those clicks — only aggregate
referral reports.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EEA you have rights of access, correction, erasure,
restriction and portability over personal data I hold about you. For the
newsletter that is the subscriber record at Buttondown. Email me and I will
action the request.
