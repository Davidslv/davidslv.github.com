About

I photograph people in London’s light: someone crossing a wet street, waiting in a doorway, singing under a colonnade. Mostly after dark, with one camera and a 35mm lens. On Sunday mornings I photograph the car meets, for the same reason.

Featured by @pure.street.photography.
